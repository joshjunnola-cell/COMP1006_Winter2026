Project Review - PHASE TWO

Challenges Encountered
======================
One of the biggest challenges for me was getting my “Add a Task” button to actually show up once a user logged in. I kept thinking it was a PHP or session issue, but 
it turned out to be Bootstrap being picky about needing a proper ul/li structure. It was such a small thing, but it completely blocked me until I figured it out.

Another challenge was setting up my SQL queries correctly. This had taken way longer than I expected. It was frustrating because the fix ended up being simple, but 
because it had taken me so long and I needed to figure out the fix to move forward it felt really great once I did get it all sorted out!

Successes Achieved
==================
Two things that stand out the most for me in the second phase. First, I’m really happy with how the dynamic navbar buttons turned out. Watching the navbar switch
between login, logout, and account options depending on the user’s state feels clean and professional, and it took some careful logic to get it right.

Second, getting the database connection issue solved was a huge win. It was holding everything up, and once it was fixed, the rest of the project finally started to
fall into place. It felt like a major breakthrough even though the fix itself was small.

Lessons Learned
===============
I learned a lot about debugging, especially when the problem isn’t where you think it is. Sometimes the issue was PHP, sometimes SQL, sometimes Bootstrap, and 
sometimes just me overlooking something small. I also learned a lot about structuring SQL queries properly, not just making them work, but making them secure and 
organized. Those two areas feel like the biggest takeaways from this project. The experience debugging was especially a major takeaway.

Next Steps / Future Improvements
================================
Realistically speaking if I were to keep building this app, the first thing I’d do is overhaul the UI styling. As I now understand what people mean when they say 
something looks “bootstrappy.” I’d like to make the design feel more custom and polished. I’d also add categories for tasks, since that would make the app more useful 
and organized for users.
